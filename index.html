Space Stone

Controls space and allows instant travel across vast distances.

Mind Stone

Grants extraordinary mental and consciousness-based abilities.

Reality Stone

Can alter the physical nature of reality itself.

Power Stone

Contains immense destructive cosmic energy.

Time Stone

Allows manipulation of time and possible futures.

Soul Stone

One of the most mysterious Stones, connected to the soul.
